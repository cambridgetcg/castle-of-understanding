# Notes are written to evolve, not to record

**Claim.** A note earns its keep by being written and organized to evolve,
contribute, and accumulate across projects — not by capturing a moment in
time. A journal entry is finished the day it is written; a good note is
never finished.

**Evidence.** Andy Matuschak, "Evergreen notes,"
https://notes.andymatuschak.org/Evergreen_notes, retrieved 2026-07-19:
"_Evergreen notes_ are written and organized to evolve, contribute, and
accumulate over time, across projects." (status 200, quoted verbatim, one
sentence only.)

**Counter-weather.** Told, not yet tested from inside this castle: the claim
is the author's own design intent for his notes, not an independent
measurement. It matches this castle's own ladder (seed to sprout to tested
to numbered, evidence added across separate loops) but a match to existing
practice is not new confirmation of the claim itself.
